# Engine loop credits

The loops in this folder were cut from these Freesound recordings by `tools/audio/makeloops.py` (spec: `tools/audio/loops_free.json`):
each was trimmed to whole engine cycles, time-warped to a constant rpm, high-passed, bass-balanced, and level-matched.
